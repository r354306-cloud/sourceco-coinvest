Read this before you apply

Who this is for, and what can go wrong.

This is for you if
  • You are an accredited investor and can leave this money alone for years
  • You want to read the documents and make your own call on each deal
  • You like plain businesses: trades, services, things with trucks
It is not for you if
  • You might need this money back in the next few years
  • You want a diversified fund that someone else manages
  • You are looking for guaranteed income or a promised return
The risks, in plain English

We would rather you hear it from us.

Every private deal carries these. If any of them is a dealbreaker, this is the wrong product, and that is fine.

  • You can lose all of it.Small companies fail more often than large ones. One bad year, one lost customer or one key person leaving can wipe out the equity.
  • Your money is locked up.There is no market for these stakes. Plan on five to seven years, with no say over when it ends.
  • You depend on the sponsor.You are a passive investor. The sponsor runs the company and makes the calls.
  • We can be paid on more than one side.SourceCo may earn fees from the sale of a company or from the sponsor in a deal you see. You will be told on each deal.
  • Past deals prove nothing about the next one.Everything on this page describes how we work, not what you will earn.
