How it works

From memo to written Pre-Check in a few days.

  • 1Click Equity Pre-Check on the memo.
    The deal code fills in for you.
  • 2Get a first answer in 1 business day.
    Yes, no, or the short list of what we still need.
  • 3Short call, then the written Pre-Check.
    What we checked, how much equity is likely, what has to be true, and when we decide.
  • 4Bid with it.
    Show the seller if you want. Once you sign, we decide by the date on the Pre-Check.
