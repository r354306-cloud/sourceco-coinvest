What is SourceCo Co-Invest?

A way for independent sponsors to line up equity for their deals, and for investors to back those deals one at a time. It is built on SourceCo's marketplace of founder-owned companies.

Who can join the waitlist?

Independent sponsors, searchers and other buyers of small companies, and investors who meet the legal bar for private deals (accredited investors).

Does it cost anything?

No. Joining is free and there is no obligation.

When does it open?

In waves, starting with SourceCo's own community. We will email you when your group opens.

I have a deal right now. Should I wait?

No. Sponsors can get a free Equity Pre-Check today on any SourceCo marketplace deal.

Is this an offer to invest?

No. This page only collects interest. Any investment would be offered only through formal documents to verified accredited investors, if and when one is available.
