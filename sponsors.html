Who sees your deal

It is about the access.

Most independent sponsors raise one call at a time from people they already know. Equity Connection puts your approved deal in front of equity providers you would not reach on your own. Each one is approved by SourceCo before they see a deal.

Private equity firms

Funds that co-invest next to sponsors

Lower middle market funds that back independent sponsors, take co-invest positions, or want deals below their fund's usual size.

  • Cheque size[$X to $X]
  • Looks forProven sponsors, platform and add-on fits
Family offices

Direct deals with patient capital

Single and multi-family offices that want direct ownership in founder-owned companies, often with longer holds.

  • Cheque size[$X to $X]
  • Looks forDirect deals, a sponsor who runs the company
Ultra-high-net-worth investors

Individuals who back the sponsor

Accredited individuals, many of them former founders and operators, who want to back a company alongside the sponsor running it.

  • Cheque size[$X to $X]
  • Looks forA business and a sponsor they understand
What it is not. The whole stack. Equity Connection fills a piece of your equity, next to your own capital, your lender and any lead investor. SourceCo does not put its own money into the deal, and every viewer signs an NDA before they see the company's name.
