Who sees your deal

It is about the access.

Most independent sponsors raise equity one call at a time, from people they already know. Co-Invest puts an approved deal in front of equity providers you would not otherwise reach.

  • Family offices. Looking for direct deals in founder-owned companies.
  • High-net-worth investors. Accredited individuals who want to back a deal next to the sponsor running it.
  • Other equity providers. Each one is approved by SourceCo before they can see a deal.
What it is not. The whole stack. Co-Invest fills a piece of your equity. Your own capital, your lender and any lead investor are still yours to line up.
